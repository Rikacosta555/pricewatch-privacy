3. How the extension works

When you click “Follow this item”, the extension reads the price shown on that page. Chrome then asks your permission to access that specific retailer’s website — nothing is granted when you install the extension, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Chrome’s settings.

A few times a day, the extension revisits the pages of the items you follow to read their current price, and sends those readings to our server. The server builds the history and decides whether a price drop is worth an email. The extension never reads pages you have not chosen to follow.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and followed items — for as long as your account exists.
  • Price history — kept while the service needs it. It records prices of public products and contains no personal data.
  • Technical diagnostic logs (rejected price readings) — 30 days, then automatically deleted.
  • After you delete your account — your email address, your followed items and your alert history are deleted immediately.

7. Affiliate links

The “Buy now” button in our alert emails links directly to the retailer’s page. It currently carries no affiliate or tracking code, and we earn no commission from it.

If that ever changes, we will say so plainly — in this policy, in the extension, and in the emails themselves — before it takes effect.

9. Cookies and tracking

The extension sets no cookies and contains no advertising or analytics trackers. Your sign-in session is stored locally in your browser, using Chrome’s extension storage, so that you are not signed out every time the browser restarts.

10. Children

PriceWatch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your data is protected by database-level access rules: the database itself enforces that you can only ever read your own followed items, rather than relying on the app to filter politely. Connections are encrypted in transit.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your rights or freedoms, we will notify you and the ICO as the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. For anything significant — a new purpose, a new provider, or the introduction of affiliate links — we will tell you by email before the change takes effect.
